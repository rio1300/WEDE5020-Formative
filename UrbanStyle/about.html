About UrbanStyle

Affordable Fashion for Everyone

UrbanStyle Clothing Store is a South African clothing retailer providing contemporary and affordable clothing, shoes and accessories.

Our target customers include teenagers, students and young adults between the ages of 16 and 35 who are interested in modern fashion and convenient online shopping.

Our goal is to make fashion shopping simple, convenient and enjoyable on both computers and mobile devices.
